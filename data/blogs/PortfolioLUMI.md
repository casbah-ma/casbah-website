# LUMI Morocco - Brand Identity - 2026

LUMI Morocco was founded as a new-generation travel and experience brand aiming to go beyond traditional travel agency positioning. The ambition was to create a brand that reflects premium execution and refined experience design, combines modern elegance with strong Moroccan identity, communicates clarity, trust, and intentionality, and is scalable across digital platforms, events, print, and physical environments. The challenge was to build a complete brand ecosystem from scratch — not just a logo — capable of supporting LUMI's long-term growth and high-end positioning.

![LUMI Morocco Logo](/portfolio/lumi-branding/lumi-logo1.png)

The project required defining a strong and differentiated brand identity, creating a recognizable visual language aligned with premium travel experiences, ensuring consistency across all touchpoints including digital, print, events, and merchandise, establishing clear brand guidelines for long-term usage, and balancing modern design with cultural anchoring in Morocco.

CASBAH delivered a full branding system beginning with brand strategy and positioning. This included the definition of LUMI's brand essence, tone, and values, positioning as a premium execution and experience design partner, with a narrative focused on clarity, precision, and meaningful connections. The logo design and identity work involved the creation of a modern, bold, and elegant logotype with strong typographic character ensuring visibility and memorability, anchoring the brand visually to Morocco while keeping international appeal.

![LUMI Brand Applications](/portfolio/lumi-branding/lumi-brand-application.jpg)

The color system development combined deep navy blues, vibrant yellow accents, and neutral tones, designed to convey sophistication, energy, and clarity, while being adaptable across light and dark environments. A comprehensive typography system was established with a primary font family for strong, expressive headlines and a secondary font family for clarity and readability in body content, creating a clear typographic hierarchy ensuring consistency across communications.

![LUMI Iconography System](/portfolio/lumi-branding/lumi-iconography.png)

The iconography and visual language included a custom icon system aligned with the brand's geometry and style, creating a visual rhythm supporting both digital and print usage. Comprehensive brand guidelines were delivered in the form of a brandbook covering logo usage and protection zones, minimum sizes and readability rules, color codes for both digital and print, typography hierarchy, and clear do's and don'ts for brand consistency. This was designed to ensure long-term coherence across all platforms and partners.

![LUMI Logo Variations](/portfolio/lumi-branding/lumi-logo-variations.jpg)

Brand applications spanned business cards and corporate stationery, marketing brochures and printed materials, event signage and large-scale visuals, merchandise including apparel, accessories, and badges, as well as digital mockups for mobile and online presence. CASBAH led the branding project end-to-end, from brand strategy and positioning definition through visual identity creation including logo, colors, and typography, to design system and guidelines development, application design across physical and digital supports, and delivery of a scalable brand ecosystem ready for growth.

The key results and impact included the creation of a strong premium brand identity from launch, high visual recognition through bold and distinctive design language, seamless consistency across all brand touchpoints, enhanced credibility with high-end clients and partners, and a long-term scalable identity system supporting digital platforms, events, and expansion. Qualitatively, the project positioned LUMI as a refined experience design brand rather than a standard travel agency, strengthened emotional connection through modern yet culturally rooted aesthetics, and provided internal teams and partners with clear branding standards.

The LUMI Morocco branding project illustrates how a well-designed identity system can act as a growth foundation rather than a simple visual layer. By combining strategic positioning, bold design choices, and rigorous brand guidelines, CASBAH delivered a complete branding ecosystem capable of supporting premium experiences, digital platforms, and international visibility. This project showcases CASBAH's ability to create brands built for long-term scalability and strong market differentiation.
