# Digital Activation of Rural Tourism in Casablanca-Settat – Building an Inclusive Tourism Ecosystem

Rural tourism operators in the Casablanca-Settat region faced multiple structural barriers to digital adoption: Very limited online presence. Absence from booking and distribution platforms. Low access to digital marketing tools. Uneven digital skill levels. Geographic and infrastructure constraints. As a result, many local accommodations, activity providers, restaurants, and cooperatives were disconnected from tourism flows and modern digital ecosystems. The mission aimed to remove these barriers by deploying a comprehensive and accessible digital transformation program adapted to rural realities.

The project aimed to create digital presence for rural tourism operators at scale, enable autonomy through simple and accessible tools, integrate operators into global online distribution channels, build digital skills through hands-on training, increase visibility, attractiveness, and commercial opportunities, and foster inclusive tourism growth across all provinces of the region.

![Rural Tourism Digital Activation](/portfolio/rural-tourism/rural-tourism-cover.jpg)

CASBAH implemented an AI-powered system enabling the rapid creation of personalized websites for each rural tourism operator. Each website was automatically generated based on the operator's activity type, customised with tailored visual and textual content, optimized for search engines and online visibility, and designed for simple management and autonomy. This allowed hundreds of operators to gain a professional digital presence in a very short time.

Operators were registered on major OTA platforms, supported in setting up optimized profiles, and integrated into global tourism marketplaces. This step connected rural tourism actors directly to international and domestic demand.

CASBAH deployed an AI-based social media content generation tool enabling operators to create engaging posts automatically, generate captions and visuals adapted to their activity, and maintain consistent online presence. Operators were trained to use the tool autonomously, transforming social media into a daily marketing channel.

A conversational AI chatbot was implemented and operators trained to use it for capturing visitor contact information, managing inbound requests, analyzing message sentiment, recommending response tone (friendly, formal, reassuring, etc.), and suggesting ready-to-use responses. This improved responsiveness and professionalism without increasing workload.

Beyond tools deployment, CASBAH delivered practical digital training sessions, hands-on workshops, and continuous support, ensuring real adoption and long-term digital autonomy.

The program covered rural accommodations (guesthouses, lodges, rural inns), leisure and nature activities (hiking, equestrian tourism, etc.), local restaurants, and artisan and terroir cooperatives across all provinces of the Casablanca-Settat region.

CASBAH acted as the full ecosystem architect and execution partner, responsible for digital transformation strategy design, AI-powered platform deployment, website factory development, marketing automation tools, chatbot system creation, training programs delivery, distribution channel integration, and ongoing support and optimization.

The project achieved over 200 rural tourism operators digitally activated, massive deployment of personalized professional websites, strong increase in online visibility across the region, integration of rural actors into global tourism platforms, improved customer engagement and responsiveness, and significant digital skill uplift among beneficiaries. The qualitative impact included reduced digital divide in rural tourism, empowered local entrepreneurs with modern tools, created a connected regional tourism ecosystem, and enabled inclusive tourism development.

This project demonstrates how technology can be used as a lever for inclusive tourism development. Rather than isolated tools, CASBAH designed a full digital ecosystem combining AI-powered infrastructure, training and adoption, marketing automation, and distribution integration, creating sustainable transformation instead of short-term digital presence. It stands as a benchmark for large-scale regional tourism digital activation programs.
