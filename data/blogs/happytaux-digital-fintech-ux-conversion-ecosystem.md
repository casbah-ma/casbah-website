# HappyTaux - Credit Brokerage - 2026

Access to credit in Morocco has long been characterized by complex processes, lack of transparency, and time-consuming administrative steps. Borrowers often face difficulty comparing banking offers and conditions, limited visibility on the best financing options, lengthy procedures to complete applications, and stress and uncertainty throughout the journey. At the same time, the credit brokerage sector remained largely traditional, relying on one-to-one physical operations that limited scalability and efficiency. The challenge was to design a fully digital platform capable of simplifying access to financing, handling large volumes of requests, optimizing conversion through UX and automation, and creating a modern fintech experience across multiple customer segments.

The project objectives centered on building a complete digital brokerage ecosystem from the ground up, designing intuitive user journeys for individuals, professionals, and businesses, digitizing the full credit request and processing lifecycle, enabling personalized offers between clients and partner banks, continuously optimizing performance through UX experimentation, and tracking user behavior while fully respecting privacy and consent.

CASBAH designed and delivered a full-stack fintech ecosystem including a promotional website and content platform with multi-segment architecture for individuals, professionals, and enterprises. The platform features service-based navigation by financing type, conversion-oriented content strategy, and SEO-optimized acquisition funnels. The client digital space provides smart, fractioned application forms with simulation and progressive data capture, real-time application status tracking, secure document upload and management, offer acceptance, and workflow progression capabilities.

The administrative back-office platform enables eligibility review pipelines, personalized financing offer creation, document request management, centralized case tracking dashboards, and performance monitoring tools. A dedicated banking interface facilitates secure file transmission to partner banks, structured collaboration workflows, and faster processing and validation cycles. UX/UI and conversion optimization efforts included detailed end-to-end user journey mapping, continuous A/B testing on key funnel steps, friction analysis and iterative improvements, and progressive UX logic to reduce abandonment.

Data and privacy-compliant analytics were implemented using Microsoft Clarity for behavior insights and heatmaps, Google Analytics for performance measurement, and CookieYes for consent-based tracking and privacy compliance. CASBAH acted as the full digital transformation and growth partner, handling platform strategy and proprietary technology development, UX/UI design and journey engineering, content structuring and funnel creation, back-office and banking workflow automation, data-driven optimization and experimentation, and continuous monitoring and performance improvement.

The platform achieved high completion rates on digital application journeys and strong user engagement thanks to frictionless UX design. The system enabled scalable processing of large volumes of credit requests and improved transparency between clients, brokers, and banks. Continuous performance improvement through testing and analytics created measurable impact. From a qualitative perspective, the project modernized the credit brokerage experience in Morocco, shifted from traditional one-to-one brokerage to a scalable digital model, enabled operational efficiency through automation, and created a fintech-ready infrastructure for future growth.

HappyTaux was not designed as a simple digital version of a traditional brokerage. The platform was built as a scalable fintech ecosystem where technology replaces operational bottlenecks, UX drives conversion and retention, data guides continuous optimization, and automation enables high-volume processing. This project illustrates how digital ecosystems can transform legacy service industries into performance-driven, scalable platforms.
