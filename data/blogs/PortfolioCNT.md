# CNT Digital Clusters Program – Digital Transformation of 80 Tourism Operators (2025)

As part of Morocco's national tourism roadmap (Feuille de Route 2026), the CNT Digital Clusters Program aimed to strengthen the competitiveness of small and medium tourism operators across multiple regions. Many local operators were facing low digital visibility, limited online distribution, lack of structured storytelling and content, weak presence on international booking platforms, and needed transformation into digitally active tourism businesses.

The project focused on improving digital visibility of local tourism operators, structuring their online presence through modern websites, boosting international bookings via OTAs like Viator, providing professional visual content including photos and videos, and transferring digital marketing skills through field training.

CASBAH delivered a hybrid large-scale digital transformation program combining multiple innovative solutions. Through the WAKATI Solution, we generated 80 AI-powered responsive, multilingual websites in English, French, Arabic, Spanish, and Italian, with integrated call-to-action buttons for WhatsApp booking, Viator links, and social media connections, all optimized for both mobile and desktop user experiences.

![CNT Digital Clusters - WAKATI websites](/portfolio/cnt-digital-clusters/cnt-image-3.jpg)

The Social Media Whisperer tool provided automated content ideas and captions specifically adapted for Instagram, TikTok, Stories, and Feed formats, helping operators maintain an active social presence. Our visual production team created 65 promotional videos in vertical format optimized for social platforms, along with professional photo libraries for each participating operator.

![CNT Digital Clusters - Responsive solutions](/portfolio/cnt-digital-clusters/cnt-image-2.jpg)

For OTA integration, we established 23 active Viator accounts with optimized product listings, professional visuals, competitive pricing strategies, and comprehensive back-office training to ensure operators could manage their international distribution channels effectively.

The CNT Academy On Tour training program brought field-based workshops directly to operators in accessible languages including Darija, English, and Amazigh. The curriculum covered essential skills such as storytelling for tourism sales, online booking optimization, social media management for beginners, and customer relationship management fundamentals.

![CNT Academy On Tour - Training program](/portfolio/cnt-digital-clusters/cnt-cover.jpg)

CASBAH led the entire digital ecosystem design and deployment, encompassing strategic digital methodology, the AI website generation platform WAKATI, comprehensive UX/UI architecture, visual production management, OTA distribution setup, and complete training program design and execution.

The program delivered impressive results with 82 websites created, 65 promotional videos produced, 23 Viator accounts activated, 21 operators trained through collective workshops, and hundreds of operators now digitally visible and commercially active across multiple regions.

![CNT Digital Clusters - Training workshops](/portfolio/cnt-digital-clusters/cnt-image-4.jpg)

The qualitative impact has been transformative, with improved online presence for previously invisible operators, providing first access to international tourism markets, establishing structured digital tools for long-term autonomy, and generating strong engagement and satisfaction from all participants.

The CNT Digital Clusters Program demonstrates how structured digital ecosystems can empower local tourism economies. By combining technology, content, distribution, and capacity building, CASBAH delivered a scalable transformation model aligned with Morocco's tourism development strategy. This project sets a benchmark for inclusive digital tourism growth across regions, positioning CASBAH as a strategic digital transformation partner capable of delivering comprehensive solutions that create lasting impact for tourism businesses at every level.
