# CNT - National Confederation of Tourism - 2025

As part of Morocco's national tourism roadmap (Feuille de Route 2026), the CNT Digital Clusters Program aimed to strengthen the competitiveness of small and medium tourism operators across multiple regions. Many local operators were facing low digital visibility, limited online distribution, lack of structured storytelling and content, and weak presence on international booking platforms. The mission focused on transforming these operators into digitally active tourism businesses.

The project objectives centered on improving digital visibility of local tourism operators, structuring their online presence through modern websites, boosting international bookings via OTAs such as Viator, providing professional visual content including photos and videos, and transferring digital marketing skills through comprehensive field training programs.

CASBAH delivered a hybrid large-scale digital transformation program that combined multiple innovative solutions. Through our AI-Generated Websites powered by the WAKATI Solution, we created responsive, multilingual websites supporting English, French, Arabic, Spanish, and Italian. Each website featured integrated CTA buttons for WhatsApp booking, Viator links, and social media connections, all optimized for both mobile and desktop user experiences.

Our Social Media Content Assistant, the Social Media Whisperer, automated content ideas and captions specifically adapted for Instagram, TikTok, Stories, and Feed formats. This tool empowered operators to maintain an active and engaging social media presence without requiring extensive marketing expertise.

The visual production component delivered 65 promotional videos in vertical format optimized for social platforms, accompanied by professional photo libraries customized for each operator. These visual assets provided operators with high-quality content to showcase their unique offerings and attract international visitors.

For OTA Integration, we focused on Viator as a key international booking platform. We created and optimized 23 active Viator accounts, complete with product listings, professional visuals, competitive pricing strategies, and comprehensive back-office training to ensure operators could manage their accounts independently.

The CNT Academy On Tour represented our commitment to capacity building through field-based workshops delivered in accessible languages including Darija, English, and Amazigh. The training program covered essential skills such as storytelling for tourism sales, online booking optimization, social media for beginners, and customer relationship management. These workshops ensured that operators could effectively leverage their new digital tools for long-term success.

CASBAH led the entire digital ecosystem design and deployment, taking responsibility for the strategic digital methodology, AI website generation platform architecture, UX/UI design, visual production management, OTA distribution setup, and training program design and execution.

The program achieved remarkable results across multiple dimensions. We successfully created 82 websites, produced 65 promotional videos, activated 23 Viator accounts, and trained 21 operators through collective workshops. Hundreds of operators are now digitally visible and commercially active, representing a significant transformation in Morocco's tourism landscape.

Beyond the quantitative metrics, the qualitative impact has been profound. Previously invisible operators now enjoy improved online presence and have gained their first access to international tourism markets. The structured digital tools we implemented provide them with long-term autonomy, and the strong engagement and satisfaction from participants demonstrate the program's effectiveness in meeting their needs.

The CNT Digital Clusters Program demonstrates how structured digital ecosystems can empower local tourism economies. By combining technology, content, distribution, and capacity building, CASBAH delivered a scalable transformation model aligned with Morocco's tourism development strategy. This project sets a benchmark for inclusive digital tourism growth across regions, proving that comprehensive digital transformation can create sustainable competitive advantages for small and medium tourism operators while contributing to broader national tourism objectives.
