# S'Tours - Full Rebranding - 2026

S'Tours is one of Morocco's main Destination Management Companies, with over 40 years of experience in the tourism industry. Over decades of operation, the company had built strong operational expertise and market credibility. However, its visual identity and brand expression no longer reflected its leadership position in the market, the evolution of modern travel and experience design, the expectations of international partners and clients, or the dynamism of Morocco's tourism sector. The challenge was to modernize the brand while preserving its heritage and trust built over more than four decades.

The project objectives were to refresh S'Tours' brand identity without losing its historical credibility, create a modern, dynamic and recognizable visual language, strengthen brand coherence across all touchpoints, build a scalable identity system adaptable to digital and physical media, and position S'Tours as a forward-looking DMC while honoring its legacy.

CASBAH deployed comprehensive brand strategy and modernization solutions, beginning with analysis of S'Tours' heritage, market positioning, and future ambitions. The team defined a refreshed brand narrative focused on connection, movement, and experience, balancing legacy trust with modern visual expression. The logo redesign and identity system included creation of a new dynamic logo symbol inspired by movement and connection, horizontal and vertical logo versions for versatile usage, a standalone icon for compact applications and brand recognition, and clear safe space and minimum size rules ensuring visual consistency.

The color system development brought a bold and vibrant color palette with core colors built around deep international blue and vibrant orange, complementary secondary colors for sub-brands and backgrounds, designed to convey reliability, energy, and modernity. The typography system featured selection of a modern, geometric sans-serif typeface for strong visual impact, clear hierarchy between display typography and body text, and optimization for both digital and print readability.

Visual language and graphic elements included custom iconography aligned with the brand's geometry, pattern systems derived from the logo icon, and graphic compositions for communication materials. The comprehensive brand guidelines (brandbook) documented logo usage rules and incorrect applications, color codes for digital and print, typography hierarchy, iconography and patterns, and applications across media, designed to ensure long-term consistency across teams and partners.

Brand applications encompassed corporate stationery including business cards, letterheads, and envelopes, presentation folders and documentation, marketing materials and brochures, signage and large-scale visuals, merchandise and promotional items, and digital assets for online presence.

CASBAH led the full rebranding process end-to-end, managing strategic brand audit and positioning, visual identity creation and modernization, logo system design and scalability rules, color and typography system development, brand guidelines production, and design of real-world brand applications.

The project delivered significant results and impact, with a modernized brand image aligned with contemporary tourism standards, strong visual differentiation in a competitive DMC market, preserved heritage while introducing a dynamic and future-ready identity, improved brand consistency across all communication channels, and enhanced perception among international partners and clients. The qualitative impact reinforced S'Tours' leadership position, increased brand memorability through bold design language, and provided internal teams with clear branding tools and standards.

The S'Tours rebranding project demonstrates how legacy tourism brands can successfully evolve without losing their core identity. By combining strategic positioning with a modern and scalable design system, CASBAH delivered a transformation that honors more than 40 years of experience, aligns the brand with today's travel and experience economy, and prepares S'Tours for future growth and digital expansion. This project highlights CASBAH's ability to modernize established institutions while preserving their heritage and market trust.
