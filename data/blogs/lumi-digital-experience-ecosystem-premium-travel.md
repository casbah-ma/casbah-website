# LUMI - Premium Travel & Events - 2026

LUMI positions itself as a premium execution partner in Morocco, with a design-led and highly structured approach to travel and event experiences. Their philosophy is to orchestrate complexity into clarity, designing journeys and events as one continuous system, from initial intent to on-ground execution. To support this positioning, LUMI required a digital platform capable of expressing a refined and premium brand identity, explaining complex service frameworks in a clear and intuitive way, showcasing Morocco's diverse destinations and experiences, highlighting a strong sustainability and responsible travel commitment, and engaging decision-makers seeking bespoke, high-quality experiences.

The project aimed to deliver a premium digital brand experience aligned with LUMI's positioning, structure MICE services around a clear operational framework, present leisure travel offerings through curated journey design, showcase Morocco's destinations in a coherent and inspiring way, integrate sustainability principles into the digital narrative, and enable scalable content management through an admin platform.

CASBAH deployed comprehensive solutions to meet these objectives. The digital platform and content architecture was designed as a narrative-driven website structured around intent, orchestration and execution. Dedicated sections were built for the MICE framework covering Scope, Control, and Execution, while complementary leisure travel framework pages addressed Curation, Flow, and Care. Destination showcases were created to highlight Morocco's major cities and landscapes, and a sustainability hub was integrated to present responsible travel philosophy and programs.

![LUMI Digital Platform - Home Feed](/portfolio/lumi-digital/lumi-home-feed.jpg)

The UX/UI and front-end development featured a premium, minimalist interface with strong visual storytelling. The responsive design works seamlessly across desktop, tablet and mobile devices, with scroll-based navigation for seamless content flow and optimized media loading for enhanced performance.

![LUMI Digital Platform - Destinations](/portfolio/lumi-digital/lumi-destinations.jpg)

A comprehensive back-office and content management system was developed, featuring an admin dashboard for managing frameworks, destinations and sustainability content. Role-based access control ensures secure content updates, while the scalable structure allows for future expansion. Analytics and compliance were addressed through user behavior tracking via Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, with consent-based analytics management powered by CookieYes.

![LUMI Digital Platform - Framework](/portfolio/lumi-digital/lumi-framework.jpg)

CASBAH acted as the end-to-end digital transformation partner, handling brand positioning and narrative strategy, UX research and journey mapping, UI design and visual identity translation, full-stack website development, content creation and SEO structuring, admin platform development, and analytics and privacy compliance setup.

The results demonstrate strong premium brand perception aligned with LUMI's positioning, clear understanding of service frameworks by visitors, enhanced storytelling around sustainability and responsible travel, improved engagement and qualified inquiries, and operational efficiency through self-managed content updates.

The LUMI digital ecosystem demonstrates how a carefully designed platform can elevate a brand from a traditional service provider to a true experience architect. By combining premium storytelling, structured service frameworks, and responsible travel principles, CASBAH delivered a flagship digital platform that reflects Morocco's richness while setting new standards for high-end travel and event management.
